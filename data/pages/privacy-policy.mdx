This Privacy Policy explains how TechToolHub ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit www.techtoolhub.online and any associated domain (the "Site"). By using the Site you agree to this policy.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** If you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address and the content of your message. We use it only to reply to you.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, referring page, pages visited and the date and time of your visit. This helps us understand which content is useful and keep the Site secure.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners use them to:

- remember preferences such as light or dark mode;
- measure traffic and how the Site is used (analytics);
- show and measure advertising.

**Your consent.** Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) are only set **after you accept** them in our cookie banner. If you reject them, Google Analytics is not loaded at all. Our cookieless page-view counter (Vercel Web Analytics, see [Analytics](#analytics)) runs for every visitor and sets no cookies. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page; withdrawing removes the analytics cookies we set. Strictly necessary storage (for example, remembering your light/dark theme and your cookie choice) does not require consent.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work correctly without them.

## Advertising: Adsterra

The Site shows banner ads served by **Adsterra**, a third-party advertising network. The ads load inside separate frames, so Adsterra can't read the content of our pages. Adsterra and its advertisers may use cookies, device identifiers and the technical data described above (such as IP address, browser and device type) to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure clicks and prevent fraud. We don't share your contact details with Adsterra. See [Adsterra's privacy policy](https://adsterra.com/privacy-policy/) for how it handles data.

We only use standard banner ads: no pop-ups, pop-unders or redirects. You can block or delete advertising cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Advertising: Google AdSense

We may also show ads through Google AdSense in the future. If we do, Google and its partners act as third-party vendors and may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other websites. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent to advertising cookies through Google's consent message; until consent is given, ads are served without personalisation.

- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- Learn more about how Google uses data at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Analytics

We use two analytics services to understand aggregate usage:

- **Vercel Web Analytics** counts page views for every visitor without cookies. Instead of a cookie, visitors are identified by a hash created from the incoming request, so it can't recognise you across websites. See [Vercel's Web Analytics privacy information](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).
- **Google Analytics** loads only if you accept analytics cookies in our cookie banner. It may set cookies and collect the technical data described above. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Affiliate links

Some links on the Site are affiliate links. When you click one, the retailer or software vendor may set a cookie to attribute a purchase to us. We do not receive your personal or payment information from these partners.

## How we use information

We use information to operate and improve the Site, respond to messages, analyse traffic, show relevant ads, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. **We do not sell your personal information.**

## Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Site (such as hosting, analytics, advertising and form-handling providers), when required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

## Data retention

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. Analytics and advertising data is retained according to the providers' own policies.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
