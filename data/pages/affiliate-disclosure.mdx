TechToolHub is free to read. To keep it that way, I earn money in two ways:

1. **Affiliate links.** Some links to products and services are affiliate links. If you click one and buy or sign up, I may earn a commission at **no extra cost to you**. These links are marked with `rel="sponsored"` and articles that contain them show a disclosure at the top.
2. **Advertising.** The site shows banner ads served by Adsterra, and may use Google AdSense in the future. Ads are labelled "Advertisement". Advertisers do not choose or approve what I write.

## My promise

- Commissions never affect scores, rankings or verdicts. I recommend tools that are not affiliate partners whenever they are the better choice.
- Vendors cannot pay for a review, a higher score or to remove criticism.
- If a vendor gives me free access to a paid plan for testing, I say so in the article.

This disclosure is provided in line with the US Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides and similar rules in other countries. Questions? [Get in touch](/contact).
