TechToolHub is free to read. To keep it that way, we earn money in two ways:

1. **Affiliate links.** Some links to products and services are affiliate links. If you click one and buy or sign up, we may earn a commission at **no extra cost to you**. These links are marked with `rel="sponsored"` and articles that contain them show a disclosure at the top.
2. **Advertising.** We display ads served by Google AdSense. Advertisers do not choose or approve what we write.

## Our promise

- Commissions never affect scores, rankings or verdicts. We recommend tools that are not affiliate partners whenever they are the better choice.
- Vendors cannot pay for a review, a higher score or to remove criticism.
- If a vendor gives us free access to a paid plan for testing, we say so in the review.

This disclosure is provided in line with the US Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides and similar rules in other countries. Questions? [Contact us](/contact).
