## Who I am

TechToolHub is researched and written by <AuthorName />. It's an independent site for people who rely on software to get work done: developers, creators, marketers, students and small-business owners. There are thousands of new tools every year and many "reviews" online are rewritten press releases. I want a place that tells you plainly what a tool does, what it costs and who it's for.

I'm a software engineer based in Sri Lanka, and I use AI, developer and productivity tools in my own work every day. I started TechToolHub to give clear, honest comparisons without the hype.

More about me on the [author page](/author).

## What I cover

- **AI Tools**: writing assistants, chatbots, image and video generators, AI research tools.
- **Developer Tools**: IDEs, AI coding assistants, hosting, databases, APIs and DevOps.
- **Productivity**: note-taking, project management, automation, calendars and focus apps.

## How I work

Every article is researched from official pricing pages, documentation and independent coverage, and compares each tool with its closest alternatives. Sources are listed at the end of each article. Articles marked **Hands-on tested** also include my own testing, with the plan I used, when, and screenshots. Articles without that badge are based on research only. Read the full [review process](/how-we-review).
