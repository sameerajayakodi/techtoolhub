## Who we are

TechToolHub is an independent review site for people who rely on software to get work done: developers, creators, marketers, students and small-business owners. There are thousands of new tools every year and most "reviews" online are rewritten press releases. We wanted a place that tells you what a tool is actually like to use.

## What we cover

- **AI Tools**: writing assistants, chatbots, image and video generators, AI research tools.
- **Developer Tools**: IDEs, AI coding assistants, hosting, databases, APIs and DevOps.
- **Productivity**: note-taking, project management, automation, calendars and focus apps.

## How we work

Every tool we review is used on real tasks, compared with its closest alternatives and scored against the same criteria. We note what we tested, which plan we used and when. Read the full [review process](/how-we-review).

## Independence

We are reader-supported. Some links on this site are affiliate links and we display ads through Google AdSense. Neither vendors nor advertisers can buy a better score, review a draft or tell us what to write. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure) for details.
