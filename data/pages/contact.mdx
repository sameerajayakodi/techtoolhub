## Before you write

- **Corrections:** include the article link and what needs fixing. We update articles quickly and note significant changes.
- **Review requests:** tell us the tool name, a link and why our readers would care. We cannot guarantee coverage, and we never accept payment for a review or a score.
- **Advertising & partnerships:** sponsored placements are always clearly labelled and never affect review scores.
