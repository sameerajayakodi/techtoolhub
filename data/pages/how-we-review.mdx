TechToolHub is a one-person site, researched and written by <AuthorName />. Here is exactly how articles are made, so you know how much weight to give them.

## 1. Research first

Every article starts with the vendor's official pricing pages and documentation, plus independent coverage. Sources are listed at the end of each article, together with the date prices were checked.

## 2. Hands-on testing, where marked

Articles with the **Hands-on tested** badge include my own use of the tool: I sign up like a normal customer (or use a vendor-provided account, which I disclose), use it for real tasks, and note the plan, version and date, with screenshots. Articles without the badge are based on research only, and the box at the end of each article says which kind it is.

## 3. Side-by-side comparison

Each tool is compared with its two or three closest alternatives on features, limits and price, so recommendations are relative to the real market and not just a feature checklist.

## 4. Scores

Hands-on reviews are scored on the same five weighted criteria:

| Criterion                 | Weight | What I look at                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Features & output quality | 30%    | Does it do the job well? How good are the results? |
| Ease of use               | 20%    | Onboarding, interface, learning curve              |
| Value for money           | 20%    | Free plan, pricing, limits, hidden costs           |
| Performance & reliability | 15%    | Speed, uptime, bugs                                |
| Support & privacy         | 15%    | Docs, support, data handling and security          |

The weighted total is the overall score out of 5:

Scores in research-based lists are an editorial judgement from published information (features, limits and price), not test results. Only scores from hands-on reviews get review markup for search engines.

## 5. Keeping articles current

Software changes fast. I re-check pricing and key features regularly and update the "Last updated" date on every article I revise.
