## 1. Hands-on testing

We sign up like a normal customer (or use a vendor-provided account, which we disclose) and use the tool for real work for at least a week. We note the plan, version and date tested in every review.

## 2. Side-by-side comparison

Each tool is compared against its two or three closest alternatives on the same tasks, so scores are relative to the real market and not just a feature checklist.

## 3. Scoring

Every review uses the same five weighted criteria:

| Criterion                 | Weight | What we look at                                    |
| ------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Features & output quality | 30%    | Does it do the job well? How good are the results? |
| Ease of use               | 20%    | Onboarding, interface, learning curve              |
| Value for money           | 20%    | Free plan, pricing, limits, hidden costs           |
| Performance & reliability | 15%    | Speed, uptime, bugs                                |
| Support & privacy         | 15%    | Docs, support, data handling and security          |

The weighted total is the overall score out of 5:

## 4. Keeping reviews current

Software changes fast. We re-check pricing and key features regularly and update the "Updated" date on every article we revise.
