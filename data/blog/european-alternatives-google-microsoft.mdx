"Digital sovereignty" has gone mainstream in Europe. Governments are moving off US software, a new European office suite launched in June 2026, and more people want their email, files and AI tools run by **European companies under European law**.

The good news: you can now replace almost every Google and Microsoft app with a European alternative, often with a free plan. Here are the best options, with country, prices and what each one replaces. Prices were checked on **3 November 2026**.

  (Germany) · **AI assistant:** Mistral Vibe (France)

## For governments and large organisations

Public bodies have their own European options too: **openDesk**, developed for Germany's Federal Ministry of the Interior by ZenDiS, and **Docs**, a collaborative editor built by France's DINUM and Germany's ZenDiS. Both aim to give administrations a sovereign alternative to US cloud suites.

## How to switch without the pain

### Start with email

Move your email first (Proton or Tuta), then set up forwarding from your old Gmail or Outlook address for a few months so you don't miss anything.

### Move files gradually

Export your Google Drive with Google Takeout or your OneDrive files, then upload to Proton Drive or Nextcloud folder by folder.

### Check your AI tools too

AI assistants see a lot of personal data. If GDPR and data location matter to you, check where your assistant stores conversations and what the new [EU AI Act rules](/blog/eu-ai-act-2026-deadlines) require.

## FAQ

### What is the best European alternative to Gmail?

Proton Mail (Switzerland) and Tuta (Germany) are the two leading options. Both offer free plans and end-to-end encryption.

### Is there a European alternative to Microsoft 365?

Yes. Nextcloud combined with Euro-Office or ONLYOFFICE covers files, chat, calendar and Office-compatible documents, and can be self-hosted or hosted in Germany.

### Is Proton in the EU?

Proton is based in Geneva, Switzerland, which is not in the EU but has strong privacy laws and is recognised by the EU as providing adequate data protection.

### Is there a European alternative to ChatGPT?

Mistral Vibe (formerly Le Chat) from France is the best-known option, with data hosted in the EU by default.
