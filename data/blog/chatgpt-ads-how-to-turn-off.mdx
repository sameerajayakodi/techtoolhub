Since February 2026, **ChatGPT shows ads** to people on the **Free** and **Go** plans. If you've started noticing "Sponsored" boxes under your answers, here's how ChatGPT ads work, what data they use, and every way to hide, limit or completely avoid them.

Information is from OpenAI's announcements and help pages as reported by the sources at the end, checked on **2 November 2026**. Menu names can vary slightly between the app and the website.

<Callout type="success" title="The short version">
  **Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education have no ads.** On the Free plan you can switch to
  an **ad-free mode with fewer daily messages**. On any plan with ads you can **turn off ad
  personalization** and **delete your ad data**, and **temporary chats show no ads**.
</Callout>

## Who sees ads in ChatGPT?

| Plan                            | Ads? | Ad-free option                                             |
| ------------------------------- | ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free                            | Yes  | Yes, in exchange for fewer daily messages                  |
| Go ($8/mo)                      | Yes  | Not offered on Go (reported); downgrade to Free or upgrade |
| Plus ($20/mo)                   | No   | n/a                                                        |
| Pro ($100/$200)                 | No   | n/a                                                        |
| Business, Enterprise, Education | No   | n/a                                                        |

Ads started in the **US on 9 February 2026** for logged-in adults, and OpenAI has since expanded them to more countries. OpenAI says it doesn't show ads to users under 18.

## How ChatGPT ads work

- Ads are **labelled "Sponsored"** and shown **separately** from ChatGPT's answer. OpenAI says ads do not influence answers.
- **No ads on sensitive topics** such as health, mental health or politics.
- By default, ads are chosen using your **current conversation, past chats and how you've interacted with previous ads**.
- Advertisers only get **aggregate statistics** (views, clicks), not your chats or personal details.

## How to manage or turn off ChatGPT ads

### Option 1: Switch to ad-free mode (Free plan)

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Manage ads**.
3. Choose **Switch to ad-free plan**.
4. Confirm **Reduce message limit**.

You'll stop seeing ads, but you get **fewer daily messages**, and **image generation and Deep Research become unavailable**. To go back, choose **Show ads** from the banner that appears when you hit the limit, or from **Manage ads** in Settings.

<Callout type="warning" title="Go plan users">
  The ad-free option is reported to be **unavailable on Go**. To remove ads you would need to switch
  back to Free (and use ad-free mode) or upgrade to Plus.
</Callout>

### Option 2: Turn off ad personalization (any plan with ads)

In **Settings → Manage ads** you can:

- turn off **Ad personalization**;
- turn off use of **past chats and memory** for ads;
- **view your ad history and topics**;
- **Delete ad data** (full deletion can take up to 30 days).

Turning personalization off **does not remove ads**: you'll still see them, just less tailored to you. It is also separate from the setting that controls whether your chats are used to train models.

### Option 3: Hide a single ad

Tap the **⋯ (More options)** menu on any ad to **Hide ad**, **Report ad**, see **About this ad** (why you're seeing it) or **Ask ChatGPT** about it.

### Option 4: Use Temporary Chat

**Temporary chats don't show ads**. They're also not saved to your history, so they are handy for one-off questions.

### Option 5: Upgrade or switch assistant

**ChatGPT Plus ($20/month)** removes ads completely and adds [GPT-6 Sol](/blog/gpt-6-chatgpt-whats-new), higher limits and extra tools. Alternatively, **Claude** and **Gemini** don't show ads on any plan; compare them in [ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini](/blog/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-gemini).

## Privacy tips

- In April 2026 OpenAI updated its privacy policy, and marketing cookies were reported to be **on by default** for Free and Go users. Review your cookie choices in ChatGPT's privacy settings.
- Don't paste passwords, banking details or other sensitive data into any AI assistant, with or without ads.
- Clearing ad data doesn't delete your chats; manage those separately in **Data controls**.

## FAQ

### Can you turn off ads in ChatGPT for free?

Yes, on the Free plan: Settings → Manage ads → Switch to ad-free plan. You'll get fewer daily messages and lose image generation and Deep Research.

### Does ChatGPT Plus have ads?

No. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education plans are ad-free.

### Do ChatGPT ads use my conversations?

By default, yes: ads can be based on your current chat, past chats and ad interactions. You can turn this off in Settings → Manage ads, and advertisers never see your chats.

### Are there ads in temporary chats?

No. Temporary chats don't show ads.
