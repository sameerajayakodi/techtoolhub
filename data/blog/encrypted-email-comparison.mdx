The difference is in what else you get: Proton bundles a whole privacy suite, while Tuta focuses on email and calendar with more of your data encrypted. ## Who each service is for

**Proton Mail** is for people who want to move most of their digital life away from Google in one go: email, calendar, files, passwords and VPN under one Swiss account. **Tuta** is for people who mainly want private email and calendar, with as much of their data encrypted as possible, and lots of addresses and custom domains on a cheap plan.

## Encryption and privacy

Both services use end-to-end encryption, so the provider can't read your messages.

- **Proton Mail** says it uses end-to-end and zero-access encryption and that "not even Proton" can read your emails. Proton is in Switzerland and says it only responds to official requests from Swiss authorities.
- **Tuta** encrypts emails "including subject lines and all attachments". Sender and recipient addresses and email dates are not encrypted. Since March 2024 it has been rolling out TutaCrypt, a post-quantum protocol using Kyber-1024. Tuta doesn't use PGP, but you can send a password-protected encrypted email to anyone.

## Features and extras

Proton is the bigger ecosystem. Proton Unlimited adds Proton VPN, Proton Pass (password manager) and Proton Drive (500 GB shared storage), and unlimited hide-my-email aliases. Mail Plus includes 10 hide-my-email aliases.

Tuta gives you more for less on email itself: its cheapest paid plan has 15 extra addresses and 3 custom domains (Proton Mail Plus: 10 and 1), unlimited calendars and labels, catch-all addresses and shared mailboxes on paid plans.
