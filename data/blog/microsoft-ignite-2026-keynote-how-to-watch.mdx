**Microsoft Ignite 2026** takes place from **17 to 20 November** at the Moscone Center in San Francisco. It is Microsoft's biggest yearly event for IT teams, developers and businesses, and it is where the company announces much of its Copilot, Azure and Windows news for the coming year.

Here is when the keynote starts in your time zone, how to watch it for free and what the published agenda suggests Microsoft will talk about. The event details below are from the official Ignite website, read on **10 November 2026**.

## How to watch Microsoft Ignite for free

Ignite has three passes:

| Pass            | Price    | What you get                                                                   |
| --------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Digital pass    | **Free** | Keynotes, sessions and "a wide range of technical content" online              |
| Hub-only pass   | $1,625   | The Hub at Moscone Center (booths, theatre sessions, lunch), 17 to 19 November |
| Conference pass | $2,325   | All in-person sessions and experiences, plus the digital venue                 |

To watch online, go to **ignite.microsoft.com**, choose **Register**, and pick the **Digital pass**. Microsoft's FAQ confirms there is "a digital event experience available online".

If you attend in person, Microsoft is also offering one free Microsoft or GitHub certification exam on site. There's also an Ignite Celebration concert with Gwen Stefani at Chase Center on the Wednesday night.

## What to expect from Ignite 2026

Microsoft doesn't publish its announcements before the keynote, so treat this section as an educated guess. It is based on the event's session tracks, the Pre-Day workshops and the speaker list.

**The session tracks** are: Agentic Business Applications, Agent Platform, Cloud & AI, Copilot, Data, Frontier Transformation, Industry, Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program, Microsoft IQ, Security and Windows. Agents appear in two of the track names alone, so AI agents are likely to be the main theme.

**The Pre-Day workshops** give more detail. They include:

- Building your first agent in **Copilot Studio**
- "Ready, secure, govern": **Microsoft 365 Copilot & Cowork**
- Building context-aware AI with **Microsoft IQ**
- Getting devices ready for AI with **Entra, Intune and Windows**
- **Windows 365** cloud PCs and **Windows Server** with AI
- Apps on **Microsoft Fabric** and AI apps on **Azure Kubernetes Service**

**The speakers** include chief executive **Satya Nadella**, Microsoft commercial chief **Judson Althoff**, **Scott Guthrie** (Cloud + AI), **Charles Lamanna** (Business & Industry Copilot), **Jacob Andreou** (Copilot), **Hayete Gallot** (Security) and Azure chief technology officer **Mark Russinovich**. The featured speakers also include the heads of **Agent 365** and **Microsoft Scout**.

Put together, the most likely news is:

- **More capable agents** for Microsoft 365 and Dynamics, and tools for building and managing them (Copilot Studio, Agent 365).
- **Copilot updates** for work, including Microsoft 365 Copilot and Cowork.
- **Security and governance** for AI agents, through Entra, Intune and Microsoft's security products.
- **Azure AI and data** news, from Microsoft Fabric to new AI infrastructure (AMD and NVIDIA are among the event's main sponsors).
- **Windows** for businesses, including Windows 365.

In past years, Microsoft has published a "Book of News" listing every Ignite announcement on the first morning. I'll update this article with the confirmed news after the keynote.

## Is Ignite worth watching if you're not in IT?

The keynote is aimed at business and IT buyers, so expect a lot of talk about "agents" and "transformation". But Ignite announcements often reach everyday tools within months: Copilot in Word, Excel and Teams, Windows features, and security changes in your work account. If you use Microsoft 365 at work, the keynote is a quick way to see what is coming to the tools you use every day.
