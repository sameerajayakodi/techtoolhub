## How to choose the right AI coding assistant

{/* Buying guide: 3–5 H3s with the criteria readers should weigh. Great for long-tail SEO. */}

### Language and IDE support

Make sure the assistant supports your main languages and editor. Most work best in VS Code.

### Privacy and code ownership

Check whether your code is used for training and whether a self-hosted or zero-retention option exists.

### Pricing

Start with a free plan, measure how many suggestions you actually accept, then upgrade.

## FAQ

### What is the best free AI coding assistant?

ByteBuddy offers the most capable free plan we tested.

### Are AI coding assistants safe to use on company code?

It depends on the tool's data policy. Choose one that does not train on your code, or a self-hosted option such as Forge AI.
