## How to choose a note-taking app

### Do you need collaboration?

If you share notes with classmates or a team, **Notion** is the strongest choice. OneNote also handles shared notebooks well.

### Do you care about owning your data?

**Obsidian** keeps notes as local files you can open in any text editor, and its paid Sync is end-to-end encrypted.

### Use two apps, not one

Many people pair a **quick-capture app** (Google Keep or Apple Notes) with a **deep-work app** (Notion or Obsidian). Capture fast, then organise later.

## FAQ

### What is the best free note-taking app?

Microsoft OneNote is the most capable completely free option. Obsidian is also free (sync is optional), and Notion's free plan covers most personal use.

### Is Notion or Obsidian better?

Notion is better for collaboration, databases and all-in-one workspaces. Obsidian is better for privacy, offline access and building a linked personal knowledge base with plain Markdown files.

### Is Evernote still worth it in 2026?

Only if you rely on its web clipper, document scanning or search inside PDFs and images. For most people, Notion, Obsidian or OneNote offer more for less.

### Which note-taking app is best for students?

OneNote (free, great for handwriting and class notebooks) and Notion (free plan, good for planning and group projects) are the two best picks for students.
