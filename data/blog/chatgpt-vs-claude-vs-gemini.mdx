ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are the three AI assistants most people choose between in 2026. All three now have capable free plans, a roughly **$20/month** paid tier and much pricier "power user" plans, so the real question is which one fits the way you work.

This guide compares them on **price, free-plan limits, flagship models, coding, research and everyday usefulness**, using each company's official plan pages and recent independent coverage (sources at the end). Prices are in US dollars and were checked in **October 2026**.

<Callout type="success" title="Quick answer">
  **Pick ChatGPT** if you want one assistant that does a bit of everything (chat, images, agents,
  voice). **Pick Claude** if your work is mostly writing, analysis or code. **Pick Gemini** if you
  already use Gmail, Docs and Google Drive, since Google AI Pro also bundles 5 TB of storage.
</Callout>

## ChatGPT: the best all-rounder

ChatGPT is still the most feature-complete assistant. OpenAI rolled out its **GPT-6** model family in September and October 2026, and ChatGPT bundles chat, image generation, voice, Deep Research, an agent that can complete multi-step tasks, and the **Codex** coding agent in one app.

The trade-off is the low end. Since early 2026 OpenAI has shown **ads on the Free and Go plans** in some markets, and the most capable models and higher limits sit behind Plus ($20) and Pro ($100/$200).

## Claude: best for writing, analysis and coding

Anthropic's Claude is popular with writers, analysts and developers for its long, well-structured answers and strong coding ability. The **free plan** already includes web search, file creation, code execution, memory and Artifacts, but it uses the Sonnet and Haiku models only.

**Claude Pro** ($20/month, or $17/month billed yearly) adds the top Opus model, **Claude Code** (Anthropic's coding agent for the terminal and IDE), Research, Projects and Claude in Chrome. **Max** starts at $100/month for 5× or 20× more usage than Pro. Claude has no budget tier and doesn't list built-in image or video generation, so it is a more focused tool than ChatGPT or Gemini.

## Gemini: best value for Google users

Google's Gemini is built into Gmail, Docs, Drive and Android, which makes it the most convenient choice if you already work in Google's ecosystem. The free tier is generous, with Deep Research, Gemini Live voice, Canvas and Gems.

**Google AI Pro** ($19.99/month) unlocks Google's newest **Gemini 4 Argon** model, a 1-million-token context window, Gemini inside Gmail and Docs, a trial of Veo video generation and **5 TB of storage**. **AI Ultra** now starts at $99.99/month, down from $249.99 before May 2026.

## Final verdict

There is no single winner in 2026, but there is a clear default for most people:

- **ChatGPT Plus** is the safest all-round choice if you want one subscription that covers chat, images, research and coding.
- **Claude Pro** is the best pick for writers, analysts and developers who care more about the quality of text and code than about extra features.
- **Google AI Pro** gives the most for your money if you already live in Google's apps, especially with 5 TB of storage included.

All three have free plans, so the smartest move is to try the same real task in each for a week before paying.

## FAQ

### Which is better, ChatGPT or Claude?

ChatGPT has more features (image generation, voice, agents and a cheaper Go tier). Claude is often preferred for long-form writing, document analysis and coding with Claude Code. For most people, the right choice depends on whether they need breadth (ChatGPT) or depth in text and code (Claude).

### Is Gemini better than ChatGPT?

Gemini is better value if you use Gmail, Docs and Google Drive, because it is built into those apps and Google AI Pro includes 5 TB of storage. ChatGPT has the broader standalone feature set.

### Are ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini free?

Yes. All three have free plans. ChatGPT's Free plan can show ads in some regions, Claude's free plan uses the Sonnet and Haiku models, and Gemini's free plan uses Google's Flash models.

### Which AI is best for coding?

Claude (with Claude Code) and ChatGPT (with Codex) both include full coding agents on their $20 plans. See our [AI coding assistants comparison](/blog/best-ai-coding-assistants) for details.
