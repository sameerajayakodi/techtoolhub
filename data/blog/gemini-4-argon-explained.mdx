**Gemini 4 Argon** is Google's new flagship AI model and the first of the Gemini 4 generation. After a cautious launch to cybersecurity partners, it reached the **Gemini app** on 13 October 2026 together with a new **Tasks** mode that keeps working on jobs while you are away from your computer.

Here's what's new, how it compares with GPT-6 and Claude, and exactly which plan you need. Facts are from Google's statements as reported by the sources at the end, checked on **2 November 2026**.

<Callout type="success" title="The short version">
  Argon is available to **Google AI Pro ($19.99/month)** and **Google AI Ultra (from $99.99/month)**
  subscribers in the Gemini app and in AI Mode in Search. It is **not on the free plan**. It answers
  more directly, handles long multi-step jobs better and powers the new Tasks mode.
</Callout>

Google also said it will **no longer release Gemini 3.5 Pro**: Argon replaces it.

## What's new in Gemini 4 Argon

### More direct answers

Google says Argon answers **more directly with less hedging**, repeats itself only when needed and shares progress more often on long jobs. In practice that means shorter, more decisive replies.

### Tasks mode: Gemini keeps working without you

The headline feature is **Tasks**. Instead of a normal chat, you hand Gemini a job and it keeps working after you leave your computer. It can use **more than 40 connected apps**, including Instacart for groceries and OpenTable for restaurant bookings, with Adobe and Shopify for business users.

Tasks is available to Pro and Ultra subscribers in **selected countries** for now, and Google says Argon is still rolling out to all Pro subscribers.

### Better at coding

Google highlights major coding improvements. Ultra subscribers on a Mac also get the **Antigravity** agentic coding interface inside the Gemini app. (Developers: see what changed for [Antigravity's free tier on 2 November](/blog/google-antigravity-free-tier-changes).)

## How does Argon compare with GPT-6 and Claude?

Google's own chart for **DeepSWE v1.1**, a long software-engineering benchmark, shows Argon at **77.9%**, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 at 74.2% and about a tenth of a point ahead of GPT-6 Astra. But Reuters notes Argon **trails on two of the four coding benchmarks** Google published, and all of these results are self-reported.

## How to get Gemini 4 Argon

1. **Subscribe to Google AI Pro ($19.99/month) or AI Ultra (from $99.99/month)**. Pro also includes 5 TB of storage; Ultra adds 20 TB and higher Argon limits.
2. **Open the Gemini app** (web, Android, iOS or Mac) signed in with that Google account.
3. **Choose Gemini 4 Argon** from the model menu if it isn't already selected. If you don't see it yet, the rollout hasn't reached your account or country.
4. **Switch to Tasks** for longer jobs you want Gemini to finish in the background.

### Gemini 4 Argon API pricing

Developers can use Argon in the Gemini API at an introductory **$2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens until 30 November 2026**, after which it doubles to **$4 and $20**.

## FAQ

### Is Gemini 4 Argon free?

No. It requires Google AI Pro or Ultra. Free users keep Google's Flash models.

### What is Gemini Tasks?

Tasks is a mode in the Gemini app where you give Gemini a job and it keeps working after you step away, using connected apps such as Instacart and OpenTable.

### Is Gemini 4 Argon better than GPT-6?

On Google's DeepSWE coding benchmark it is marginally ahead of GPT-6 Astra, but it trails on other coding benchmarks Google published. For most people the deciding factor is the ecosystem: Gemini fits best if you live in Gmail, Docs and Drive.
