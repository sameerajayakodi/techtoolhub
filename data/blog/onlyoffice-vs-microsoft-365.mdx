## Who each suite is for

**ONLYOFFICE** is for people and teams who mainly need document, spreadsheet, presentation and PDF editing, want to avoid per-user subscriptions, or want to keep files on their own server. **Microsoft 365** is for people who live in Outlook and Teams, share files with organisations that use Microsoft, or want Copilot built into Word and Excel.

## Word, Excel and PowerPoint compatibility

ONLYOFFICE uses DOCX, XLSX and PPTX as its native formats and says you can work with them "without worrying about broken layouts or missing elements". It also opens ODT, ODS, ODP and CSV. Microsoft 365 is the reference for these formats, so files made in Word or Excel will always look right in Word or Excel.

## Collaboration, PDF and AI

Both support real-time co-editing and comments. In ONLYOFFICE, co-editing needs a cloud: ONLYOFFICE DocSpace, or a connected service such as Nextcloud, ownCloud or Dropbox. Microsoft 365 adds Outlook and OneDrive. {/* TODO: confirm which business plans include Teams on Microsoft's business pricing page. */}

ONLYOFFICE's desktop editors include PDF editing and fillable PDF forms, and can convert PDFs to editable documents.

For AI, Microsoft 365 includes Copilot, with more usage on Personal, Family and Premium than on a free account. ONLYOFFICE AI Tools is an optional layer that works with your choice of AI provider, and the Enterprise edition can run local models fully offline.

**Winner:** Microsoft 365 for the all-in-one workplace; ONLYOFFICE for PDF forms and choosing your own AI.

## Privacy and self-hosting

ONLYOFFICE Docs can be self-hosted on your own server or private cloud, and its desktop editors work without any cloud account ("You decide where your data lives"). The editors are open source under AGPLv3. Microsoft 365 is a Microsoft-hosted cloud service.

**Winner:** ONLYOFFICE.

## FAQ

### Is ONLYOFFICE really free?

The ONLYOFFICE Desktop Editors are free for personal and professional use on Windows, macOS and Linux, and the DocSpace Startup cloud plan is free for up to 3 admins and 2 GB of storage. Paid plans add storage, admins, branding and support.

### Is ONLYOFFICE cheaper than Microsoft 365?

For individuals, yes: the desktop editors are free, while Microsoft 365 Personal costs $99.99 a year (checked November 2026). For teams, ONLYOFFICE DocSpace Business costs $20 per admin a month with unlimited users. TODO: compare with Microsoft 365 Business Standard per user.

### Can ONLYOFFICE open Word and Excel files?

Yes. DOCX, XLSX and PPTX are its native formats, and it also opens ODT, ODS, ODP and CSV.

### Does ONLYOFFICE have AI like Copilot?

ONLYOFFICE AI Tools is an optional AI layer for text generation, translation, OCR and chat, using the AI provider you choose. Microsoft 365 includes Copilot, with usage limits that depend on your plan.
